How to Contract With Us

SDVOSB Set-Aside

Contracting officers can award contracts directly to Swalmy under SDVOSB set-aside provisions (FAR 19.14). Simplified acquisition threshold applies for sole-source awards.

Sole-Source Awards

SDVOSB sole-source contracts up to $4 million for goods and services. Contact us for capability statements and past performance documentation.

Competitive Bids

We actively monitor and respond to opportunities on SAM.gov, state procurement portals, and municipal bid boards.

Subcontracting

Available as a subcontractor to prime contractors seeking to meet small business subcontracting plan goals.

Past Performance

Capability statements and past performance documentation available upon request for qualified contracting officers and prime contractors.
