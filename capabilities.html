Our Capabilities

Emergency Housing

Rapid-Deploy Housing Solutions

When disaster strikes, communities need shelter immediately. Swalmy provides rapid-deployment emergency housing units designed for quick setup and durable long-term use. Our housing solutions serve FEMA operations, military installations, and state emergency management agencies.

  • Disaster relief temporary housing
  • Workforce housing for remote installations
  • Transitional housing for displaced populations
  • Modular units scalable to any operation size
  • Climate-appropriate configurations available

Shelters & Storage

Durable Shelter & Storage Systems

Swalmy supplies robust shelter and storage structures for government facilities, military bases, and municipal operations. From utility sheds to large-scale storage facilities, our solutions are built to withstand demanding environments and meet federal specifications.

  • Equipment storage structures

  • Weather-resistant designs for all climates

Straps & Rigging

Military-Grade Straps & Tie-Down Systems

Our industrial strap and rigging solutions are manufactured to meet or exceed military specifications. From vehicle transport tie-downs to cargo securing systems, Swalmy provides the reliable restraint solutions that government and military operations depend on.

  • Ratchet straps and cam buckle straps
  • Limit straps for vehicle suspension
  • Cargo nets and securing systems
  • Custom-length and custom-rated solutions
  • Mil-spec materials and construction
