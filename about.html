About Swalmy

A disabled minority veteran-owned business built on service, integrity, and mission readiness.

Our Story

Swalmy was founded with a clear purpose: to bring the discipline, reliability, and mission-first mentality of military service into the government contracting space. As a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business, we understand what it means to deliver under pressure — because we've done it in uniform.

Today, Swalmy serves as a premier provider of emergency housing, shelter systems, storage solutions, straps, and industrial goods to federal agencies, military installations, state governments, and municipalities across the United States. We bridge the gap between manufacturers and government buyers, ensuring that critical supplies reach the people who need them — on time and on spec.

Our disabled minority veteran ownership isn't just a certification — it's the foundation of who we are. We bring firsthand understanding of government operations, military logistics, and the urgency that emergency response demands.
