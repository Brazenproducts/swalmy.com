Industrial Overspray Containment for Government Facilities

Government facilities — from military vehicle depots to federal fleet maintenance shops — operate spray booths and paint application systems that generate significant overspray. Without proper filtration, overspray creates fire hazards, OSHA violations, and environmental compliance failures. Swalmy supplies the full range of overspray filtration products needed to keep government and military paint operations safe, compliant, and running efficiently.

As a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business registered in SAM.gov, Swalmy is positioned as a preferred SDVOSB source for filtration supply. Contracting officers can issue set-aside purchase orders, blanket purchase agreements, or indefinite-delivery contracts to Swalmy for ongoing filtration supply needs — simplifying the procurement process while fulfilling small business contracting goals.

  • Downdraft pit flooring filters for vehicle paint booths
  • HEPA intake ceiling filters for positive-pressure booths
  • Overspray containment systems for open-air industrial finishing
  • Activated carbon odor control panels for enclosed finishing areas
  • Bulk filter media supply for high-volume operations

Overspray Filtration Product Categories

Swalmy supplies filtration for every type of spray booth configuration and open-air industrial finishing application used by government agencies and military operations.

Spray Booth Exhaust Filters

High-efficiency fiberglass and polyester exhaust filters for crossdraft, semi-downdraft, and side-draft spray booths. Available in standard and custom sizes. Designed for paint overspray, primer, and clear coat applications. Filter efficiency up to 98% at 10 microns.

  • Fiberglass filter media — standard and high-capacity
  • Polyester filter pads for extended service life
  • Galvanized exhaust filters for heavy-duty industrial use
  • Available in rolls, pads, and pre-cut panels

Downdraft Booth Floor Filters

Pit and raised-floor filtration for vehicle paint booths used in military and government fleet maintenance. Downdraft floor filters capture overspray as it falls, preventing accumulation in floor grates and exhaust plenums. Critical for NFPA 33 compliance in enclosed vehicle finishing areas.

  • Polyester downdraft filter rolls — standard and heavy-duty
  • Custom-cut floor panels for vehicle bay dimensions
  • High-capacity media for armored vehicle and heavy equipment
  • Grate covers and filter frames available

Intake & Ceiling Filters

Positive-pressure booth integrity starts at the intake. Swalmy supplies ceiling and intake filters that prevent contamination from entering the spray environment, protecting finish quality and reducing rework. HEPA-rated options available for critical military and aerospace finishing operations.

  • Ceiling filter media rolls for positive-pressure booths
  • HEPA ceiling modules for aerospace and precision finishing
  • Pre-filter panels for high-dust environments
  • Aluminum filter frames and retaining clips

Open-Air Overspray Containment

Not every government painting operation happens in a booth. Field maintenance, bridge and infrastructure painting, and large vehicle surface preparation generate airborne overspray that must be contained. Swalmy supplies open-air overspray containment systems for outdoor and semi-enclosed operations.

  • Overspray containment film and sheeting
  • Perforated wind screens for open-air finishing
  • Portable filtration walls and spray shields
  • Environmental containment systems for field operations

Government & Military Applications

Overspray filtration is a recurring supply need across a wide range of government operations. Swalmy serves all of them.

Military Vehicle Maintenance Depots

Combat vehicles, tactical trucks, and support equipment require regular surface preparation and repainting to maintain operational readiness. Swalmy supplies exhaust and floor filters for military vehicle paint booths at depots and forward bases, supporting TACOM, LOGCAP, and similar logistics commands.

Naval Aviation & Aircraft Maintenance

Aviation maintenance facilities demand the highest filtration performance. Aircraft finishing operations use specialized coatings — primers, topcoats, anti-corrosion systems — that generate fine mist overspray requiring HEPA-grade containment. Swalmy supplies HEPA ceiling and exhaust filters meeting military finishing specifications.

Federal Fleet Operations

GSA fleet facilities, postal service vehicle centers, Border Patrol maintenance yards, and other federal fleet operations run spray booths for vehicle refinishing. Swalmy provides a reliable, SDVOSB-sourced filter supply that keeps fleet paint operations in compliance and on schedule.

Infrastructure & Public Works

Bridge painting, water tower coating, facility repaints, and municipal infrastructure projects generate significant overspray that must be contained under EPA and state environmental regulations. Swalmy supplies open-air containment systems and portable filtration for public works and infrastructure finishing operations.

Military Base Facilities Management

Base facilities management contracts (facilities maintenance, custodial, and repair) often include spray painting operations for buildings, equipment, and grounds infrastructure. Swalmy serves facilities management prime contractors as a subcontractor or direct supplier for SDVOSB set-aside overspray filtration supply.

State & Municipal Maintenance Shops

State DOT maintenance depots, municipal public works facilities, transit authority maintenance yards, and county equipment shops all operate spray booths for fleet and infrastructure maintenance. Swalmy's SDVOSB status qualifies for state and local government small business set-aside programs in most jurisdictions.

Compliance

OSHA, NFPA, and EPA Standards for Government Spray Operations

Government and military facilities must operate spray booths in compliance with multiple overlapping regulatory frameworks. Improper filtration is the most common cause of compliance failures — and the easiest to correct with the right supplier.

Swalmy's overspray filtration products are sourced from manufacturers whose products meet or exceed the following standards:

  • OSHA 29 CFR 1910.94 — Ventilation standards for spray finishing operations, including requirements for filter efficiency and replacement schedules
  • NFPA 33 — Standard for Spray Application Using Flammable or Combustible Materials, including filter media flammability requirements
  • EPA NESHAP 40 CFR Part 63 Subpart HHHHHH — National Emission Standards for spray finishing at area sources
  • MIL-PRF specifications — Available for military-specification finishing environments on request

Compliance documentation, product data sheets, and filter efficiency testing results are available upon request for all product categories. Swalmy can assist contracting officers in identifying the correct product specification for their facility's configuration and compliance requirements.

Procurement Options for Overspray Filtration

Swalmy offers multiple contract vehicles to simplify overspray filtration procurement for government buyers.

SDVOSB Set-Aside Purchase Orders

Contracting officers can issue SDVOSB set-aside purchase orders for overspray filtration supplies under FAR 19.14. Simplified acquisition threshold applies. No competitive quotes required for sole-source awards up to the applicable threshold.

Blanket Purchase Agreements

Establish a BPA with Swalmy for ongoing filtration supply needs. BPAs simplify recurring filter replacement procurement by eliminating the need for individual purchase orders on each order. Ideal for facilities with monthly or quarterly filter replacement schedules.

Indefinite Delivery / Indefinite Quantity

For larger installations with multi-year filtration supply requirements, Swalmy can fulfill IDIQ contract orders. We work with prime contractors and program offices to establish delivery schedules aligned with facility maintenance cycles.

Subcontracting to Prime Contractors

Prime contractors with facilities maintenance, environmental, or base operations contracts can subcontract filtration supply to Swalmy to meet SDVOSB subcontracting plan goals. We provide teaming letters and capability statements on request.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from government contracting officers and facilities managers about overspray filtration procurement from Swalmy.

Can Swalmy supply non-standard filter sizes?

Yes. Swalmy can source custom-cut and non-standard filter sizes for legacy spray booth equipment. Government facilities often operate older equipment with non-standard filter dimensions. Provide us with your booth specifications and we will source the correct filtration media.

What lead times should we expect?

Standard catalog overspray filters typically ship within 2–5 business days. Custom sizes and specialty media have lead times of 7–14 business days depending on quantity and specification. Swalmy can work with your facility's maintenance schedule to establish a delivery timeline that avoids operational downtime.

Does Swalmy provide filter replacement schedules?

Yes. Swalmy can provide recommended replacement schedules based on your facility's usage volume, coating type, and OSHA compliance requirements. We also provide filter change documentation templates for facilities that require maintenance recordkeeping under their base operations contract.

Can Swalmy supply filters through existing GSA vehicles?

Contact us to discuss current GSA schedule participation and alternative procurement vehicles. For facilities not on GSA schedule, direct SDVOSB set-aside purchase orders under FAR 19.14 are the simplest procurement path. Our contracting team can assist with the appropriate mechanism for your agency's requirements.
