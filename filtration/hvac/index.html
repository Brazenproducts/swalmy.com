Berry Amendment Compliance

Berry Amendment Compliant HVAC Filter Media for DoD Procurement

The Berry Amendment (10 USC § 4862) requires the Department of Defense to procure fiber-based products — including HVAC filter media — that are manufactured in the United States from domestically produced materials. Non-compliant HVAC filters sourced from foreign-manufactured filter media cannot be used in DoD contracts without a waiver.

Swalmy supplies HVAC filters with Berry Amendment compliant filter media for all DoD HVAC procurement. This means contracting officers and facilities managers at military installations can source HVAC filters through Swalmy without Berry Amendment compliance risk. We provide the following documentation on request for all DoD HVAC filter orders:

  • Certificate of Conformance — confirming domestic manufacture of filter media and components
  • Country of Origin documentation — tracing fiber and media production to US sources
  • Berry Amendment compliance statement — signed certification for contracting officer file
  • Manufacturer data sheets — confirming MERV rating, filter efficiency, and media composition

Berry Amendment compliance is particularly critical for installations under direct DoD facilities management contracts, LOGCAP task orders, and base operations support (BOS) contracts. Swalmy's compliance documentation package satisfies contracting officer requirements for Berry Amendment adherence on HVAC filter line items.

HVAC Filter Selection by Facility Type

Choosing the right MERV rating for each government facility type ensures compliance, cost efficiency, and occupant health protection.

MERV 8 — Administrative & Office Facilities

Standard commercial filtration meeting ASHRAE 62.1 minimums. Captures pollen, dust mites, mold spores, and larger particles. Appropriate for general-use government office buildings, federal courthouses, and administrative facilities with good baseline air quality.

MERV 11 — High-Occupancy Government Buildings

Enhanced filtration for facilities with high occupant density or public access — social service offices, veterans' service centers, immigration facilities, and high-traffic federal buildings. Captures fine dust, auto emissions, and lead dust at 65–85% efficiency.

MERV 13 — Military Facilities (UFC 3-410-01)

DoD Unified Facilities Criteria 3-410-01 establishes MERV 13 as the baseline for general military facility HVAC filtration. Captures bacteria, smoke particles, and fine combustion products at 90%+ efficiency. Required for barracks, dining facilities, and most base buildings.

MERV 16 — Command Centers & Data Facilities

High-efficiency filtration for sensitive facilities where particulate contamination affects equipment or operations — tactical operations centers, IT server rooms, communications facilities, and sensitive compartmented information facilities (SCIFs).

HEPA — Medical & Laboratory Facilities

True HEPA filtration (99.97% at 0.3 microns) for military treatment facilities, VA medical centers, research laboratories, and biosafety-sensitive environments. Required for negative pressure isolation rooms and biological safety cabinets in government healthcare settings.

Activated Carbon — Industrial & Maintenance Areas

Odor control and chemical vapor reduction for vehicle maintenance bays, fuel storage areas, paint and coating operations, and industrial work areas where chemical off-gassing affects adjacent occupied spaces. Combined MERV/carbon options available.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Swalmy supply non-standard HVAC filter sizes?

Yes. Many government and military facilities operate legacy HVAC equipment with non-standard filter dimensions. Swalmy can source custom-cut filters in virtually any size. Provide your existing filter dimensions and we will source the correct media at the appropriate MERV rating.

Do Swalmy HVAC filters meet ASHRAE 52.2 testing standards?

Yes. All MERV-rated filters supplied by Swalmy are tested and rated per ASHRAE Standard 52.2 (Method of Testing General Ventilation Air-Cleaning Devices). MERV ratings and efficiency curves are available in product data sheets provided with each order.

What lead times should government buyers expect?

Standard MERV 8, 11, and 13 filters typically ship within 2–5 business days. MERV 16 and HEPA filters ship within 5–10 business days. Custom sizes have 7–14 business day lead times. Swalmy can work with your facility's replacement schedule to pre-position inventory for just-in-time delivery.

How does Swalmy handle large-scale installation supply?

For military installations with hundreds of HVAC units, Swalmy offers quantity pricing, consolidated pallet shipments, and scheduled delivery programs. We can work directly with the installation's facilities management contractor or with the contracting officer's representative to coordinate supply logistics for large filter replacement programs.
