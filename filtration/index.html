One SDVOSB Source for All Government Filtration Needs

Government and military facilities operate dozens of systems that require regular, compliant filtration — spray booths, HVAC systems, vehicle maintenance bays, industrial process equipment, and more. Managing multiple vendors for these recurring supply needs is inefficient and creates compliance gaps. Swalmy provides a single, SDVOSB-certified source for all industrial filtration procurement, simplifying the contracting process for facilities managers and contracting officers alike.

As a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business registered in SAM.gov, Swalmy qualifies for SDVOSB set-aside purchase orders under FAR 19.14. Sole-source awards, blanket purchase agreements, and IDIQ delivery orders are all available contract vehicles. We provide compliance documentation, product data sheets, and filter change schedules on request for all filtration categories.

Berry Amendment

Berry Amendment Compliant Filtration for DoD Procurement

The Berry Amendment (10 USC § 4862) requires the Department of Defense to purchase fiber-based products — including filtration media — that are manufactured in the United States from domestically produced materials. This applies directly to HVAC filter media, spray booth filter pads, paint arrestor fibers, and other filtration products containing US-origin textiles and fibers.

Swalmy supplies Berry Amendment compliant filter products across all filtration categories. For DoD contracting officers and facilities managers at military installations, this means you can consolidate filtration procurement with a single SDVOSB vendor and meet Berry Amendment requirements without waivers. Swalmy provides certificates of conformance, country of origin documentation, and signed Berry Amendment compliance statements for all DoD filtration orders upon request.

  • HVAC filters — Berry compliant pleated filter media, domestically manufactured
  • Overspray filters — Berry compliant fiberglass and polyester filter media
  • Paint arrestors — Berry compliant fiber-based arrestor pads
  • Certificates of Conformance provided for all DoD orders
  • Country of Origin documentation on request
  • Signed Berry Amendment compliance statement for contracting officer file
