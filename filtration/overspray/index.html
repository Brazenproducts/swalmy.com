BowTie Spray Booth Filters & Industrial Overspray Containment

Swalmy supplies BowTie spray booth filters and a full line of industrial overspray filtration products to government agencies and military installations across the United States. BowTie filters — manufactured by Walk Industrial — use a patented bow-tie shaped filtration media that delivers superior capture efficiency compared to standard flat-panel fiberglass arrestors, with lower pressure drop and longer service life.

Government facilities that operate spray booths — military vehicle maintenance depots, federal fleet operations, aircraft maintenance hangars, and public works facilities — need reliable, compliant filter supply to meet OSHA 1910.94 and NFPA 33 requirements. Swalmy provides that supply as an SDVOSB-certified source, enabling contracting officers to issue set-aside purchase orders under FAR 19.14 without competitive quotes up to the applicable sole-source threshold.

  • BowTie exhaust filters — up to 98% efficiency at 10 microns
  • Fiberglass and polyester paint arrestor pads — standard and high-capacity
  • Downdraft pit floor filters for vehicle paint booths
  • HEPA intake ceiling filters for positive-pressure booths
  • Activated carbon odor control panels
  • Open-air overspray containment systems for field operations
  • Custom sizes available for legacy and non-standard booth configurations

Overspray Filter Product Categories

Swalmy supplies filtration for every spray booth configuration and open-air industrial finishing application used by government and military operations.

BowTie Exhaust Filters

The BowTie filter's patented shape creates turbulent airflow through the media, maximizing overspray particle contact and capture efficiency — up to 98% at 10 microns. Available in standard and custom sizes for crossdraft, semi-downdraft, and side-draft booth configurations.

  • Standard sizes: 20×20, 24×24, 20×25
  • High-capacity rolls for continuous media systems
  • Custom cut for non-standard booth openings
  • NFPA 33 compliant filter media

Paint Arrestor Pads

Flat-panel fiberglass and polyester paint arrestor pads for standard side-draft and cross-draft spray booths. Economy and high-capacity options to match usage volume and replacement schedule. Galvanized steel media available for heavy industrial and military applications.

  • Fiberglass arrestor pads — standard and high-capacity
  • Polyester pads for extended service intervals
  • Galvanized steel media for heavy-duty use
  • Available in rolls, pads, and pre-cut panels

Downdraft Floor Filters

Pit and raised-floor filtration for vehicle paint booths used in military and government fleet maintenance. Captures overspray before it reaches floor grates and exhaust plenums. Critical for NFPA 33 compliance in enclosed vehicle finishing areas.

  • Polyester downdraft rolls — standard and heavy-duty
  • Custom-cut panels for vehicle bay dimensions
  • High-capacity media for armored vehicles
  • Grate covers and retaining frames available

HEPA Intake & Ceiling Filters

Positive-pressure booth integrity starts at the intake. HEPA-rated ceiling and intake filters prevent contamination from entering the spray environment, protecting finish quality for critical military and aviation finishing operations.

  • HEPA ceiling modules — 99.97% at 0.3 microns
  • Pre-filter panels for high-dust environments
  • Aluminum frames and retaining clips included
  • Available for MIL-SPEC finishing environments

Government & Military Applications

Military Vehicle Maintenance Depots

Combat vehicles, tactical trucks, and support equipment require regular repainting for operational readiness. Swalmy supplies exhaust and floor filters for military vehicle paint booths at depots and forward operating bases, supporting TACOM and similar logistics commands.

Aviation Maintenance Facilities

Aircraft finishing demands HEPA-grade containment. Swalmy supplies HEPA ceiling and exhaust filters for aircraft maintenance hangars at military air bases, naval air stations, and federal aviation facilities, meeting military finishing specifications.

Federal Fleet Operations

GSA fleet facilities, postal service vehicle centers, Border Patrol maintenance yards, and other federal fleet operations run spray booths for vehicle refinishing. Swalmy provides SDVOSB-sourced filter supply that keeps fleet paint operations in compliance and on schedule.

Public Works & Infrastructure

Bridge painting, water tower coating, and facility repaints generate significant overspray that must be contained under EPA and state environmental regulations. Swalmy supplies open-air containment systems for public works and infrastructure finishing operations.

Berry Amendment

Berry Amendment Compliant Overspray Filter Media for DoD

The Berry Amendment (10 USC § 4862) requires the Department of Defense to procure fiber-based products manufactured in the United States from domestically produced materials. This applies directly to spray booth filter media — including fiberglass filter pads, polyester arrestor media, and downdraft floor filter rolls — purchased under DoD contracts and task orders.

Swalmy supplies overspray filtration products with Berry Amendment compliant filter media for all DoD procurement. Military vehicle maintenance depots, aviation maintenance facilities, and base operations contracts that include spray booth filter supply must source Berry compliant filter media. Swalmy's compliance documentation package satisfies contracting officer requirements for Berry Amendment adherence on all filter line items.

  • BowTie and fiberglass filter media — domestically manufactured from US-origin fibers
  • Polyester downdraft and exhaust filter media — Berry Amendment compliant
  • Certificate of Conformance provided for every DoD order
  • Country of Origin documentation on request

OSHA, NFPA & EPA Standards for Spray Operations

All overspray filtration products supplied by Swalmy meet or exceed the following regulatory standards for government and military spray finishing operations:

  • OSHA 29 CFR 1910.94 — Ventilation requirements for spray finishing operations, filter efficiency and replacement schedules
  • NFPA 33 — Standard for Spray Application Using Flammable or Combustible Materials, filter media flammability requirements
  • EPA NESHAP 40 CFR Part 63 Subpart HHHHHH — National Emission Standards for spray finishing at area sources
  • MIL-PRF specifications — Available for military-specification finishing environments on request

Compliance documentation and product data sheets are provided at no charge for all product categories.
